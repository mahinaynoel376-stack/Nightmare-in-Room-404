NIGHTMARE IN ROOM 404 - GAME SCRIPT
============================================================================================
Every line of text in the game, in the order the player meets it: the story scenes, the
  clues, the bosses, the monsters' incident reports, the ending, and the short messages.
  Taken from the game's code (mostly src/main/java/nightmare404/story/Story.java), so it
  matches what's on screen.

How each kind of line is written here:
  NAME: ...             someone speaking (Kai, the Principal, Subject Zero)
  KAI (thinking): ...  Kai's thoughts, shown in italics
  [PAGE: title]         something written down, shown on lined paper
  (no label)            narration
  [HOW TO PLAY]         a tip, shown in gold
  >> ...                a short notice at the bottom of the screen


============================================================================================
PROLOGUE - ROOM 404 (FLOOR 4)
============================================================================================

--- The opening (no words: Kai wakes up) ---

  Black. Somewhere in the dark, a clock:  ...tick.   ...tick.
  Kai's eyes open a crack, and fall shut again. Then they open properly: Room 404, dark.
    He's asleep at his desk, face down on his arms, breathing. z z z
  He stirs. Lifts his head. Sits up with his eyes still shut, then opens them, looks back
    over his shoulder at the empty room, and stands up in the aisle.
  (It can't be skipped.)

--- The game begins ---

  Room 404. The lights are off. On the board, in someone else's handwriting: STAY IN YOUR
    SEAT.
  KAI: ...I fell asleep? Where is everyone?
  KAI: The clock says 11:59. It said 11:59 when I closed my eyes.
  On the teacher's desk lies the janitor's logbook. Most of its pages have been torn out.
  [PAGE: Janitor's logbook, last entry]
      11:40 PM. Footsteps on the fourth floor again. Going up to check Room 404. If you're
        reading this, don't stay late. Go home.
  KAI (thinking): Too late for that. I need to get out of here: down the stairs and out the
    front doors.
  [HOW TO PLAY] W A S D to walk, Shift to run (only for a few seconds: then you're out of
    breath and have to walk). E, Space or Enter to read on. J opens your journal.
  [HOW TO PLAY] Each floor has clues hidden in its side rooms. Every clue you find before
    the floor's boss fight takes 20% off the boss's health. The boss holds the key to the
    way down.

--- Going outside the hallways for the first time ---
Kai: the whole place is a mess and reality is twisted.
Kai: And what is that, are those monsters?!
-- something cold drips onto Kai's shoulder. He looks up --
-- a blob of black goo (the Ceiling Blob) drops from the ceiling, splats onto the floor beside him, and lunges --
-- Tut level: the first fight, with the controls explained --
--- After Tut Level ---
Kai: Wow, that was insane.. *panting. Atleast I can somewhat fend them off..
Kai: Let me find the missing pages on the Janitor's logbook, maybe that'll help me understand whats going on here

FLOOR 4: THE CLASSROOMS

--- Quest: The Janitor's Logbook (2 clues, optional: each one found before the boss fight takes 20% off its health) ---

  -- Torn logbook page, found in Room 402 --
  [PAGE: Janitor's logbook, Oct 3]
      Kids keep staying late in 404. I send them home at ten. In the morning their desks are
        empty, and nobody remembers their names. Not even their teachers.

--- Reading the first missing page ---
Kai: Oct 3.. kids staying late in 404? That's MY classroom.
Kai: Nobody remembers their names.. not even the teachers? That can't be right.
Kai: *looks back at the empty desks* Whose seats were these..

  -- Torn logbook page, found in Room 403 --
  [PAGE: Janitor's logbook, Oct 9]
      The attendance sheets are going blank: names fading right off the paper. I'm staying

Kai: I've got a really bad feeling about those stairs.

--- The boss: Brute (West Stairs) ---

  >> The way down is chained shut. The janitor's key must be on this floor.   (trying the
    way down without the key)

--- Reaching the West Stairs ---
Kai: The stairs down.. blocked. Of course they are.
Kai: Okay, think. The janitor had the keys. If I find him, I find the way down.
-- for a moment, the boulder on the stairs seems to breathe --
Kai: ..Please don't be what I think it is.

--- Walking up to the boulder (without both pages) ---
-- a huge boulder sits in the middle of the stairs down, wedged between the rails --
-- half buried in the rock: a ring of keys --
Kai: It won't budge.. not even a little.
Kai: Those keys.. they look like the janitor's. Maybe his missing pages say what happened up here.
[HOW TO PLAY] Find the janitor's two missing pages on this floor, then come back and read them to the boulder.
>> The boulder won't budge. Logbook pages found: 1 of 2.   (every time after that)

--- Reciting the logbook to the boulder (with both pages) ---
Kai: I found your pages. All of them. Let me read you your own words.
Kai: "Oct 3. Kids keep staying late in 404. In the morning, nobody remembers their names."
Kai: "Oct 9. I'm staying up here tonight to see who's doing it. My keys are on my belt."
Kai: "11:40 PM. Footsteps on the fourth floor again.. If you're reading this, don't stay late. Go home."
-- the boulder shudders. Its cracks glow orange, and something inside the rock groans awake --
-- the boulder splits open and the Brute stands up out of it --

  -- Before the fight (always with both pages: they're what wakes it) --
  KAI: Those keys... You're the janitor. You stayed up here to look for them.
  The Brute flinches. For a moment it looks almost human.

--- After the Brute (it drops nothing: beating it clears the way down) ---
Kai: *panting* It just.. fell apart. Like it was made out of the stairs.
Kai: I'm sorry. I think you were only trying to help us.
-- the rubble settles and rolls aside. The stairs down are clear --
Kai: Okay. Down we go. Don't look back, Kai.
>> The way down is clear.

Kai: ..It wants me to go back to the library?

   Checkout cards found: 1 of 3.   (every time after that)

--- Walking up to the Whisperer (intangible, without all three cards) ---

WHISPERER: ...______, Oct 2... ______, Oct 5... don't let them go... ______...
Kai: She's reading out names.. but they're all blacked out. She can't remember them either.
Kai: The head librarian.. still reading them, so they won't be lost for good.
[HOW TO PLAY] Find the three checkout cards in the library. Their names will fill in her blanks.
>> She keeps whispering names you can't make out. Checkout cards found: 2 of 3.   (every time after that)

--- Reading her the names (with all three cards) ---
Kai: I found the cards. All three. You don't have to read them alone any more.
Kai: Oct 2, Oct 5, Oct 8. Night Class. The Last Bell. I'll read every name with you.
-- Kai reads the names out, one by one. The whispering falters, then stops --
-- the Whisperer turns to face you, and for the first time, she looks solid --

  -- Before the fight --
  KAI: I know their names now, too. You can let them rest.
  The Whisperer shrieks, and every shelf in the library rattles.

--- After the Whisperer ---
Kai: It's quiet again. But a different kind of quiet.. lighter.
-- far off at the east end of the floor, the haze over the stairs thins and drifts away --
>> The Whisperer falls. Something glints where it stood...
-- the whispering stops for good. Three checkout cards drift down where she floated, and every
   blank is filled in: every name, written out in full --
KAI (thinking): She was holding on to their names. I'll hold on to them now.
Kai: Every name.. finally written down where nobody can rub it out.
Kai: The east stairs.. the OTHER end of the building. Great. *sigh*
>> Found: The three checkout cards / +1 First Aid Kit, +1 Old Textbook, +1 Hall Pass

THE GROUND FLOOR: THE OFFICES

  The ground floor. Through the windows: the quadrangle, and beyond it, the gates.
  KAI (thinking): The front doors are in the middle of the hall. Almost there.
  [HOW TO PLAY] This floor's clues: three books. A staff handbook in the Faculty Room, a

  [PAGE: Staff Handbook, Rule 12]
      No student is to remain in the building after midnight. Students who do will be dealt

        not discuss them. Do not look for them. Do not remember them. - The Principal
  >> Found with it: +1 Red Pen

Kai: "Do not remember them." The Principal ORDERED it?!
Kai: They didn't just forget those students.. they made themselves forget.

  -- Attendance register, found in Records Room --
  [PAGE: Register, Room 404]
      Every name on the list has been struck through. At the bottom, in fresh ink: F L O R.

  [PAGE: Yearbook: Room 404]

THE QUADRANGLE: THE LAST STAND
============================================================================================

--- Stepping outside (first time) ---

  Cold air. The quadrangle is silent, and every gate in the fence is chained shut.
  The slime trail crosses the grass and fades out somewhere near the south gate.
  Then, behind you, something tears itself out of the earth.
  KAI: The trees... They're alive.
  [HOW TO PLAY] SPACE to focus: time slows, and you can type. Type the word over a tree to
    strike it. SPACE again to move. Focus runs out, so pick your moments.
  [HOW TO PLAY] Walls, the bus and trees stop your strikes; fences, benches and bushes
    don't. Keys 1-4 use a First Aid Kit, an Old Textbook, a Red Pen or a Hall Pass.

--- First steps on the grass ---
Kai: Fresh air.. I almost forgot what it felt like.
Kai: Every gate's chained.. there has to be another way out of here.
-- the trees shudder, and their roots start tearing out of the ground --
Kai: Oh come ON! Can't ANYTHING in this school just stay normal?!

--- While the trees are standing ---

  >> Under the leaves by the south gate, something is breathing.   (walking close to the
    heap of leaves by the south gate, once)
  >> The Hollow Oak falls. You catch your breath: +10 HP. 3 trees left.   (each tree felled;
    the numbers change)
  >> You lose your focus. Catch your breath before you try again.
  >> Not enough focus yet: keep moving.
  (Old Textbook: thrown at the nearest monster in sight, "BOOK", 30 damage)
  >> Nothing in sight to throw it at.   (no monster in sight)
  >> Red Pen: your next strike hits twice as hard.
