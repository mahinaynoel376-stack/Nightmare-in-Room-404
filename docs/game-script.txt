NIGHTMARE IN ROOM 404 - GAME SCRIPT
============================================================================================
Every line of text in the game, in the order the player meets it: the story scenes, the
  clues, the bosses, the monsters' incident reports, the ending, and the short messages.
  Taken from the game's code (mostly src/main/java/nightmare404/story/Story.java), so it
  matches what's on screen.

How each kind of line is written here:
  NAME: ...             someone speaking (Flor, the Principal, Subject Zero)
  FLOR (thinking): ...  Flor's thoughts, shown in italics
  [PAGE: title]         something written down, shown on lined paper
  (no label)            narration
  [HOW TO PLAY]         a tip, shown in gold
  >> ...                a short notice at the bottom of the screen

PROLOGUE - ROOM 404 (FLOOR 4)
============================================================================================

--- The game begins ---

  Room 404. The lights are off. On the board, in someone else's handwriting: STAY IN YOUR
    SEAT.
  FLOR: ...I fell asleep? Where is everyone?
  FLOR: The clock says 11:59. It said 11:59 when I closed my eyes.
  On the teacher's desk lies the janitor's logbook. Most of its pages have been torn out.
  [PAGE: Janitor's logbook, last entry]

        reading this, don't stay late. Go home.
  FLOR (thinking): Too late for that. I need to get out of here: down the stairs and out the
    front doors.
  [HOW TO PLAY] W A S D to walk, Shift to run. E, Space or Enter to read on. J opens your
    journal.
  [HOW TO PLAY] Each floor has clues hidden in its side rooms. Every clue you find before

    way down.

THE QUADRANGLE: THE LAST STAND
============================================================================================

--- Stepping outside (first time) ---

  Cold air. The quadrangle is silent, and every gate in the fence is chained shut.
  The slime trail crosses the grass and fades out somewhere near the south gate.
  Then, behind you, something tears itself out of the earth.
  FLOR: The trees... They're alive.
  [HOW TO PLAY] SPACE to focus: time slows, and you can type. Type the word over a tree to
    strike it. SPACE again to move. Focus runs out, so pick your moments.
  [HOW TO PLAY] Walls, the bus and trees stop your strikes; fences, benches and bushes
    don't. Keys 1-4 use a First Aid Kit, an Old Textbook, a Red Pen or a Hall Pass.

--- First steps on the grass ---
Flor: Fresh air.. I almost forgot what it felt like.
Flor: Every gate's chained.. there has to be another way out of here.
-- the trees shudder, and their roots start tearing out of the ground --
Flor: Oh come ON! Can't ANYTHING in this school just stay normal?!

--- While the trees are standing ---

  >> Under the leaves by the south gate, something is breathing.   (walking close to the
    heap of leaves by the south gate, once)
  >> The Hollow Oak falls. You catch your breath: +10 HP. 3 trees left.   (each tree felled;
    the numbers change)
  >> You lose your focus. Catch your breath before you try again.
  >> Not enough focus yet: keep moving.
  >> Old Textbook: the next hit is halved.
  >> Red Pen: your next strike hits twice as hard.
  >> Hall Pass: everything out here stands still for a moment.

--- After the first tree falls ---
Flor: *panting* One down.. they're slow, but there's so many of them.
Flor: Keep moving, Flor. Type fast. Don't stop.

--- Halfway through the trees ---
Flor: Every root I cut.. they all lead the same way. Toward the south gate.
Flor: Something down there is pulling the strings.

--- The last tree falls: Subject Zero wakes (if every clue was found) ---

  The last tree falls. Every root in the quadrangle goes still.
  By the south gate, the heap of dead leaves sits up. Every root you cut leads back to it.
  SUBJECT ZERO: ...STAY... IN... YOUR... SEAT...
  FLOR: Subject Zero. The one that broke out of pod 2.
  FLOR: I remember all of them. And I'll remember you.
  [HOW TO PLAY] Subject Zero's words are longer. When the ground cracks around you, get out
    of the way: SPACE to stop focusing, then run.

--- The last tree falls: Subject Zero wakes (if some clues are missing) ---

  (The same, except Flor's fifth line is:)
  FLOR: Nobody's forgetting me tonight.

--- Facing Subject Zero ---
Flor: So you're the first one.. the one everybody forgot the longest.
Flor: *grips her pen* I'm not sitting back down. Not for you, not for anyone.

--- Once Subject Zero is hurt ---

  >> Subject Zero calls up its saplings through the gates!

Flor: More of them?! It's calling them in through the gates!
Flor: Focus.. focus.. one word at a time.
-- the ground cracks open under Flor's feet --
Flor: Move, move, MOVE!

--- The monsters outside ---

  HOLLOW OAK  -  the quadrangle's trees (6)
      The groundskeeper planted a tree for every student who never went home. Every spring
        there are more of them.
      - Janitor's logbook, last entry
      * Root Snare: Roots burst out of the ground and drag at your ankles.
      * Branch Lash: A heavy branch whips across the grass.
      * Leaf Storm: A gust of rotten leaves, sharp as paper.
      * Drink Deep: It sinks its roots and drinks. Its bark knits back together.

  SUBJECT ZERO  (boss)  -  by the south gate
      Subject Zero broke the glass of pod 2 on day 40. It was the first of them. It has been
        forgotten the longest.
      - Lab report, Project REMEMBER
      * Crushing Grip: It closes every hand it has around you.
      * Many Mouths: Every mouth bites at once.

  SAPLING  -  called up by Subject Zero
      * Switch: A whippy little branch, right across the shins.

THE ENDINGS
============================================================================================

--- Good ending: every clue found (8 of 8) ---

  Subject Zero comes apart into roots, dead leaves and old paper: pages from the register,
    and every name on them is still there.
  FLOR: I remember them. The janitor. The names on the cards. Subject X. All of them.
  The sky over the south gate turns grey, then gold. Inside, the clock in Room 404 finally
    ticks over.
  On Monday the register for Room 404 is full. Every name is back. Yours too.
  Ending screen:  REMEMBERED  -  You found every clue, and nobody was forgotten.

--- After the good ending ---
Flor: The janitor. The names on the cards. Subject X.. I'll say them every day if I have to.
Flor: Nobody gets forgotten. Not tonight. Not ever again.

--- Bad ending: some clues missing ---

  Subject Zero comes apart into roots and dead leaves. The chain on the south gate falls
    away.
  FLOR: I'm out. I'm actually out.
  Dawn comes. You walk home and sleep through the whole day.
  On Monday, your seat in Room 404 is gone. Nobody can remember who used to sit there.
  Ending screen:  FORGOTTEN  -  Find all 8 clues to remember everyone.

--- After the bad ending ---
Flor: Did I.. forget something? Someone?
Flor: *stares at the empty seat in Room 404* Whose desk was that..
